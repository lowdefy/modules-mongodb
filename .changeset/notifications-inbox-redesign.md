**The inbox is redesigned.** The `all` page fills the screen with a rail of views and types (each with its unread count), a list of up to 1000 notifications that scrolls on its own, and a reading pane. The header shows the unread count, a **Sound** switch, and **Mark all as read**, which names the count ("Mark 12 as read") whenever a view, type, search or date narrows it. A notification can be marked unread again from the pane. Below tablet width the rail becomes a View selector and the list and pane take turns. Every colour follows the app's theme and dark mode. The notifications module now declares `@lowdefy/modules-mongodb-plugins`.
