The support module has proper pages for the people who report problems. **My tickets** lists every ticket a user has, open and resolved, newest activity first, with the key, title, type, status and when it last changed, a dot when the team has replied since they looked, a search box for the title or key, and filters for status and type. Each ticket now opens on its own page at a clean address, such as `/support/PEL-1715`, with a header showing the key, type, status and when it was opened, the whole conversation and the reply box. Links to a ticket, including the button in the reply notification, use that address. When the app's assistant files or follows up a ticket, it now names the ticket as a link to that page instead of plain text.
