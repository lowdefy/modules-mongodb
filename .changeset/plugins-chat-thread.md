**New `ChatThread` block.** A two-party conversation thread: own messages on the right, the other side on the left with avatars, day pills, grouping of one author's messages within five minutes, linked URLs, and image or file attachments. It is its own scroll container and stays at the bottom while the reader is there. See `docs/plugins/chat-thread.md`.
