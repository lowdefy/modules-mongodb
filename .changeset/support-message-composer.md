The reply box under a ticket and the message box on the New report form look like a comment box now: one frame with the text on top, your attached images and PDFs as small thumbnails you can remove, and the attach button, a hint and Send along the bottom. You can paste an image straight into the text or drop a file onto the box, and Ctrl or Cmd with Enter sends. The limits are the same: up to five images or PDFs of 10 MB each.
