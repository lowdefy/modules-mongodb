**New `MessageComposer` block.** One bordered message box: the text on top, the attached images and PDFs as small removable thumbnails with upload progress, and along the bottom an attach button, a short hint and the send button. Pasting an image or dropping a file onto the box attaches it, and Ctrl or Cmd with Enter sends. See `docs/plugins/message-composer.md`.
